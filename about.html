Professional Summary

A technically adept and proactive Architectural Technologist based in Ottawa, Ontario, specializing in residential low-rise and mixed-use mid/high-rise projects.

With expertise in CAD software such as AutoCAD, Revit, 3DS Max, SketchUp and Twinmotion, I bring a meticulous approach to design, supported by a solid understanding of the Ontario Building Code and sustainable practices.

I thrive in collaborative environments, working seamlessly with multidisciplinary teams to combine technical precision with creative problem-solving. My commitment to excellence ensures projects are completed on time, within budget and to the highest standards.

Beyond work, I channel my creativity through photography, graphic design, biking, exploring the outdoors and playing piano.
